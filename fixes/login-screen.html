TROUBLESHOOTING

Login Screen

If a login screen appears when you launch the game, check security software and network access.

Possible Causes

  • Antivirus/security software
  • Network/DNS restrictions
  • Authentication issues

How To Fix

Step 1

Test whether your antivirus is blocking Aeris; do not leave protection disabled permanently.

Step 2

Try 1.1.1.1 / WARP and retry.

Step 3

Restart the launcher and retry authentication.

Step 4

If it remains, open a support ticket with the exact screen/error.
