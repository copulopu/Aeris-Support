Frequently Asked Questions

The answers people usually need before opening a support ticket.

How do I install Aeris?
Use the Downloads page to get the current release, then follow How to Install.
How do I fix Error 2502?
Follow the Error 2502 / 2503 guide and the linked video. Restart Windows and retry after correcting installer permissions.
Why won't Aeris launch?
Check Launcher Not Opening and Build Doesn't Launch. Verify your build path and files first.
Why is my screen black?
Check display/graphics settings and verify the build files. Include a screenshot when contacting support.
How do I change my username?
Use the account controls available in your Aeris setup. If the option is unavailable, ask support.
How do I contact support?
Use the Support page or join the Aeris Discord community.
