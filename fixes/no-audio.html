TROUBLESHOOTING

No Audio

If you cannot hear anything in your game, install the required Microsoft runtime and restart your PC.

Possible Causes

  • Missing runtime component
  • Windows audio configuration
  • Build/runtime issue

How To Fix

Step 1

Download the Microsoft DirectX End-User Runtime.

Step 2

Install it and complete setup.

Step 3

Restart your PC.

Step 4

Launch Aeris and test audio. If it still fails, create a support ticket with your audio setup and any errors.
